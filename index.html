        Why can't I set letter spacing, gradients or shadows?
        
Statements are rendered to PDF by an engine that supports a subset of CSS. We tested each
          option against the real renderer and only offer the ones that genuinely come out the other
          side. Letter and word spacing, transparency, gradients and drop shadows are all silently
          dropped, so there is no control for them — a slider that did nothing would be worse than
          its absence.
